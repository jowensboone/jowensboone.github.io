I'm a PhD student in Social-Cultural Psychology at the University of Texas at El Paso (UTEP), where I'm a member of the [Race, Ethnicity, Neuroscience, and Health Lab](https://renhlab.weebly.com/) (PI: Dr. Hannah Volpert-Esmond). My research sits at the intersection of race and policing — I'm interested in how cognitive processes shape public judgments about police use of force and legitimacy, and how we can use psychophysiological measures (EEG/ERP, EMG) to understand the mechanisms behind those judgments.

**Research Interests**
- Race and policing
- Vicarious and direct discrimination
- Use of psychophysiological measures (EEG/ERP, EMG)
- Collective action and linked fate
- Race perception and categorization

**Education**
- Ph.D. in Social-Cultural Psychology (ABD), University of Texas at El Paso
- M.S. in Experimental Psychology, Western Illinois University
- B.A. in Psychology (minors in Criminal Justice and Sociology), Arcadia University
